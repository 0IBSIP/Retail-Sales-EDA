📊 Data Analytics — Task 1

Retail Sales EDA

🔎 Project Overview

This project is completed as Task 1 of the Data Analytics Internship.

The project focuses on performing Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a retail sales dataset to identify sales trends, customer behaviour patterns, product performance, and relationships between numerical variables.

The analysis transforms raw retail data into meaningful insights that can support better business and marketing decisions.

---

🎯 Objective

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Understand the structure and quality of the retail sales dataset.
- Perform data cleaning and preprocessing.
- Calculate important descriptive statistics.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Study customer demographics such as age groups and gender.
- Identify the Top 10 best-selling products.
- Analyze revenue generated by product categories.
- Identify relationships between numerical variables using correlation analysis.
- Create an additional visualization to discover a non-obvious insight.
- Derive actionable business recommendations from the analysis.

🔬 Exploratory Data Analysis Performed

1. Initial Dataset Inspection

- Dataset shape
- First few records
- Column names
- Data types
- Missing-value analysis

2. Data Cleaning

The dataset was cleaned and prepared for analysis by:

- Converting order dates into a proper datetime format.
- Handling missing numerical values.
- Handling invalid age values.
- Standardizing categorical values such as gender.
- Checking and removing duplicate records.

3. Descriptive Statistics

Statistical analysis was performed for numerical variables using:

- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard Deviation

4. Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart was created to analyze how sales changed month by month.

5. Quarterly Sales Trend

Quarter-wise sales were analyzed to identify broader seasonal and business trends.

6. Customer Age Group Analysis

Customers/orders were grouped into age categories to understand the distribution of the customer base.

7. Gender Analysis

Gender distribution was visualized to understand the composition of customers/orders.

8. Top 10 Best-Selling Products

Products were ranked according to total quantity sold to identify high-demand products.

9. Revenue by Product Category

Total revenue was calculated for each product category and visualized using a bar chart.

10. Correlation Heatmap

A correlation matrix was created using numerical variables to identify positive and negative relationships between variables.

11. Additional Visualization

An additional visualization was created to investigate the relationship between discount percentage and profit, helping identify potential effects of discounts on profitability.

💡 Business Insights

The EDA helps identify:

- Periods of higher and lower sales.
- Customer demographic patterns.
- Products with high demand.
- Product categories generating greater revenue.
- Relationships among sales, quantity, price, discount, profit, and other numerical variables.
- Potential impact of discounting on profitability.

---

🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. Inventory Optimization
   Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand products identified through the Top 10 product analysis.

2. Category-Based Strategy
   Give greater marketing and inventory attention to high-revenue product categories while reviewing underperforming categories.

3. Seasonal Sales Planning
   Use monthly and quarterly sales trends to plan inventory, promotions, and marketing campaigns during high-demand periods.

4. Customer Segmentation
   Develop targeted marketing campaigns based on customer age-group and gender distribution.

5. Discount Management
   Monitor the relationship between discounts and profit to ensure promotional offers support revenue growth without unnecessarily reducing profitability.
